## Programme qualification

This programme track is justified by six proposed team workstreams, formal approval checkpoints, phased delivery spanning more than one quarter, and progressive onboarding of existing and new AI services. The initiative is a learning and planning exercise that models an enterprise programme; the repository remains on its personal profile, and no real bank policy or regulatory framework is asserted.
